Tracklists

Dig your tracks

using Spotify's hidden parameters

Tracklists turns your Spotify library into a playground: filter thousands of songs by mood, energy or tempo, then save the result as a playlist.

Load your library

based on your playlists and liked tracks

Every playlist you follow and every song you liked, gathered in one place. Big libraries welcome: tens of thousands of tracks load in a few minutes.

Play with parameters

Like danceability, chill or BPM

Slide the filters to keep only what fits the moment: a slow Sunday, a dance floor, a run at 160 BPM.

Discover all the genres

behind your tracks

Spotify tags artists with precise genres. Pick a few, from french house to bossa nova, and find songs you forgot you had.

Free and open source

No ads, nothing to install: log in with Spotify and start digging. The code is on GitHub.
